# Folio journey checks

Use a fresh browser profile for the first visit, then keep that profile for the persistence checks. Check the original Gallery composition at desktop widths of 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels, and narrow widths of 390 and 320 pixels. These are observable user journeys, not tests of internal markup.

1. **Discover a book.** Open the site, inspect the centered introduction, five-book hero, and six-book shelf. Open a cover and verify its title, author, description, and reading action. Return to the collection and open a different title. Every original cover identity should remain recognisable; no content should be clipped or overlap the navigation.
2. **Find something specific.** Search by a complete title, part of an author's name, and a genre. Enter a query with no matches, then clear it. Apply Fiction, Ideas, and Escape filters and return to the full collection. The full collection should contain every book shown in any category, and an empty result should offer a clear way to continue.
3. **Build a library.** Save a title from its card, verify saved feedback, then open its details and confirm the same state. Save a second title, visit the library, remove one title, and refresh. Only the remaining title should persist. Remove the final title and verify the empty state. Complete this journey using the keyboard as well as pointer input.
4. **Read through chapter boundaries.** Open each title in the reader. Read forward through its opening and continue into chapters two and three. Go backwards across a chapter boundary, navigate directly to a chapter if provided, and reach the final page. Previous and next controls should become unavailable only at the actual beginning and end. Confirm that page changes reveal fresh, ordered prose rather than repeated or skipped paragraphs.
5. **Keep a reading place.** Stop partway into a continuation chapter, return to the catalog, reopen the title, and refresh the page. The promised reading position should survive. Try a second title and confirm that its position is independent. Adjust available reading settings, then cross a chapter boundary and confirm the settings continue to apply.
6. **Move between views from lower on the page.** Scroll to lower collection actions and open search, book details, the library, or the reader. The destination's useful content and keyboard focus should be visible without manual scrolling through empty space. Close or return using visible controls and browser history where supported.
7. **Read comfortably on a small screen.** Repeat opening a book, saving, searching, and reading at 390 and 320 pixels. Check navigation labels, long titles, text-size controls, and bottom pagination. There should be no page-wide horizontal scrolling, hidden reading controls, or content requiring hover. With reduced motion enabled, transitions should preserve function and reading position.

Catalog content check completed: six fictional titles, three original demo chapters each, 33 paragraphs per title, and 1,996–2,082 words per title. Covers retain their original names and artwork structure. No ratings, bestseller claims, accounts, or external purchase flows are supplied by the data module.

## UI regression journeys

- Open search directly, enter a query, open a result, and return using browser Back and the book's back link. The query, matching results, visible search field, and useful focus should survive. Repeat after reloading the search URL.
- Save a book as a guest, open My Library, and remove it using the keyboard. Focus should remain on a useful visible control when the library becomes empty.
- Check shelves, search, related books, and saved books at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels. Covers, captions, and save controls should align. Use real coarse-pointer emulation when measuring touch targets, and test both system appearances.
- Reach the final reader spread by turning pages and by moving the progress slider to the end. Both paths should show completion consistently while reopening the book preserves the same passage.
- Open reader contents from both the toolbar and chapter label. Expanded states should agree, and dismissing contents or appearance should restore useful focus. Check desktop, phone, and short landscape layouts.
- While a reader is open, navigate to an unknown or malformed book URL. The old reader must close, background scrolling must recover, and the error page must be visible and keyboard accessible.
- At 320, 390, and 430 pixels, verify the compact single-row header and its navigation menu. Open every destination, repeat the current destination, dismiss with Escape and an outside tap, tab beyond the menu, and resize across the mobile breakpoint. Hidden links must not retain focus. Search and account actions must close the menu. Scroll to the last shelf and footer; status-bar space must stay backed by a solid surface, invitation copy must have its own column, and footer text must align as one stack. Repeat with a shorter viewport, enlarged text, and simulated safe-area insets; inspect actual screenshots as well as overflow metrics.

## Floating material checks

- Scroll covers and text beneath the floating navigation and search. Labels, active destinations, focus rings, and account actions must remain readable in light and dark appearances. Open the account dialog and check its fields and close button at 320 pixels and with the on-screen keyboard.
- Open reader contents and appearance in paper, sepia, and night. The floating controls should share one material per group; book pages must remain opaque. Check panel dismissal, keyboard focus, page turns, and the progress slider at phone, tablet, desktop, and short landscape sizes.
- Enable reduced transparency, increased contrast, forced colors, and reduced motion separately. Glass must become opaque for transparency/contrast preferences; controls and selected states must stay distinguishable, with no elastic motion under reduced motion. Where a browser does not support a preference, verify the available fallback directly. Disable backdrop-filter support and check that solid surfaces still separate controls from content.
